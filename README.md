# Kavodax (CAGD) 0→1 GTM & Commercial Execution Toolkit

A comprehensive Go-To-Market framework designed to transition Kavodax from regulatory validation (FINTRAC MSB + Bank of Canada PSP registration) to commercial volume leadership in stablecoin-powered B2B cross-border payments.

---

## Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

Traditional cross-border business payments suffer from slow settlement times (3–5 business days), high intermediary banking fees, and unpredictable FX spreads (2.5%–4.0%). 

Kavodax leverages **CAGD** (CAD Global Dollar) to provide Canadian enterprises with sub-minute, compliant, low-cost international settlements.

### The 3 Core Pillars of CAGD Commercial Positioning
1. **Speed & Capital Efficiency:** Instant to sub-5 minute settlement vs. 3–5 day SWIFT holds.
2. **Margin Protection:** 70%+ reduction in FX markups and correspondent wire fees.
3. **Regulatory Security:** Built natively on FINTRAC MSB and Bank of Canada PSP frameworks, providing institutional safety for traditional CFOs.
